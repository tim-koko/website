## Greater Efficiency through Self-Services and Automation

Complex infrastructure, manual processes and a lack of standards made it difficult for Raiffeisen Switzerland to deliver new applications. Together with Raiffeisen Switzerland and [Puzzle](https://www.puzzle.ch), tim&koko therefore developed the internal developer platform “Concierge”. The platform brings together technical capabilities, software templates and standardized development paths, so-called golden paths, in a central portal based on Red Hat Developer Hub. Through automated self-services, development teams can provision the resources they need on their own, without having to know the underlying infrastructure in detail. As a first step, an MVP was built that automatically provisions a new Spring Boot service including a database within 15 minutes at most. Following the successful proof of concept, Concierge is being continuously extended with further features, integrations and golden paths.

## About the Client

Raiffeisen is the second-largest banking group in Switzerland and the Swiss retail bank closest to its customers. It has more than two million cooperative members and over three million client relationships, including more than 200,000 companies in Switzerland. The 208 legally independent Raiffeisen banks, organized as cooperatives, are locally rooted throughout Switzerland and are members of Raiffeisen Switzerland Cooperative. Raiffeisen Switzerland Cooperative holds the strategic management and supervisory function for the entire Raiffeisen Group. The Raiffeisen Group offers private individuals and companies a broad range of financial products and services. Its core business areas include, in particular, mortgages, wealth management and pension advice, as well as corporate banking.

## Role of tim&koko

tim&koko contributed to the architecture of the platform and took on the role of Product Owner. In this role, tim&koko was responsible for the Concierge backlog and, together with Raiffeisen Switzerland, steered the prioritization and further development of the platform in line with the needs of the development teams.
