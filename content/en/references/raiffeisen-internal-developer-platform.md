## Greater Efficiency through Self-Services and Automation

Complex infrastructure, manual processes and a lack of standards made it difficult for Raiffeisen to deliver new applications. Together with Raiffeisen and [Puzzle](https://www.puzzle.ch), tim&koko therefore developed the internal developer platform “Concierge”. The platform brings together technical capabilities, software templates and standardized development paths, so-called golden paths, in a central portal based on Red Hat Developer Hub. Through automated self-services, development teams can provision the resources they need on their own, without having to know the underlying infrastructure in detail. As a first step, an MVP was built that provisions a new Spring Boot service including a database automatically within 15 minutes at most. Following the successful proof of concept, Concierge is being continuously extended with further features, integrations and golden paths.

## About the Client

Raiffeisen is the third-largest banking group in Switzerland and is organized as a cooperative. The independent Raiffeisen banks are united in Raiffeisen Switzerland Cooperative, headquartered in St. Gallen. Raiffeisen Switzerland is responsible for the strategic direction of the group and provides central services to the Raiffeisen banks, including the operation and further development of the IT platforms.

## Role of tim&koko

tim&koko contributed to the architecture of the platform and took on the role of Product Owner. In this role, tim&koko was responsible for the Concierge backlog and steered the prioritization and further development of the platform in line with the needs of the development teams.
